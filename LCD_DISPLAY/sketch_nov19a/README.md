Hello to anyone who is going to read this,

This is how to LCD-message-display for beginners 

Well, follow this wiring  format

LCD Pin	Function	Arduino
1	VSS	GND
2	VDD	5V
3	VO	Potentiometer middle pin
4	RS	2
5	RW	GND
6	E	3
11	D4	4
12	D5	5
13	D6	6
14	D7	7
15	LED+	5V (always through a resistor)
16	LED-	GND

And that's it!! I'll say don't give up because your message was not displayed; 

There might be wiring not plugged in issues, or the code might have an error

Lastly, just make sure to check if the LCD is showing brightness; that's how you will get it started first, and the rest comes later

Good Luck!!
